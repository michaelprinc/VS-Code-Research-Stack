# GitHub Copilot editor integration

Use reviewed tools through an optional assistant client.

## Setup

Check the active profile for GitHub.copilot and GitHub.copilot-chat; Chat may be bundled. Use the editor's account flow and check organization policy. The toolkit does not collect tokens or sign you in.

## First task and readiness

The inventory reports IDs/versions visible to this Extension Host. Installed does not confirm account access. List local MCP tools in your assistant client, approve a small read and invoke read_document. Agent mode enables at most 128 tools per session; the toolkit's Tools preset keeps the surface goal-shaped, and its details view shows the estimated size of those tool definitions in the model's context.

## Troubleshooting and limits

Not detected may mean disabled, another profile/host or absent. Resolve companion-version conflicts against your editor rather than forcing a downgrade. Authentication, subscription and policy remain Not verified. If the client reports a tool-limit error, reduce the toolkit preset and disable duplicate or unused servers rather than raising the limit; tool selection also becomes less reliable well below the ceiling.
