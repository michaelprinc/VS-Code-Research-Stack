# Support and feedback

Research Stack Toolkit 0.3.10 is a public preview. Report ordinary defects and feature requests at https://github.com/michaelprinc/VS-Code-Research-Stack/issues. Do not post credentials, private research documents or sensitive diagnostics in public issues.

The **Report a problem** command creates a local report and opens it for your review. It can prepare an email in your mail application after confirmation; you decide whether to send it. Review the report and remove sensitive content before sharing: automatic redaction cannot guarantee removal of every private detail.

For suspected security vulnerabilities, follow SECURITY.md.
