## Research task and ground truth

The proposed question was: *Does a fictional sunlight intervention consistently improve seedling growth compared with shade?* Study A, a synthetic two-page PDF, says a seven-day, nonrandomized pilot observed 20 seedlings per group and mean heights of 18 mm in sunlight and 12 mm in shade. Page 2 says the sunlight group received more water, a possible confounder; the sample and single room limit generalization. Study B, a synthetic Markdown follow-up, says a randomized, equal-watering replication reported 14 mm in sunlight and 15 mm in shade at seven days, with small samples and uncertainty. These are invented fixtures, not scientific findings. A separate manually authored Markdown note summarizes study A for the index.

The expected defensible conclusion is that the pilot found an association but the replication did not reproduce the advantage; neither observation proves the watering confounder caused the pilot difference or establishes a general causal effect. The later freshness test deliberately revised the follow-up values to 13 mm and 16 mm **after** the model completed its task. The original follow-up text was retained as `follow-up-before-change.md.bak`.

## Implications and next tests

- PDF parsing and provenance worked on a simple text-based, two-page document. Test a real complex PDF and a scanned PDF separately before relying on coverage; OCR is not established here.
- The index is a local SQLite/BM25 prototype. Indexing Markdown notes about a PDF does not make those chunks direct PDF-page evidence: the pilot claim in this test links to `pilot-notes.md`, not the PDF itself. A reviewed extraction-to-text bridge with durable source/page mapping would be required for a continuous PDF-to-index workflow.
- Reindex changed sources before trusting freshness flags or exports. Before reindex, the test showed a changed file's previously indexed evidence still marked current. A live source-hash check or clearer UI wording could reduce this risk.
- The unsupported-PDF result used an application error inside an MCP result whose protocol `is_error` flag was false. Verify client handling of `resultState` to avoid interpreting this as successful indexing.
- A future end-to-end test should select the Full research workflow preset in a separate trusted disposable workspace, verify the lab server is actually offered, then have Qwen perform index → search → resolve → claim → report. Test external citation providers, Zotero, report writing, backup/restore, notebook execution and adversarial or large corpora separately. None is established by this test.
