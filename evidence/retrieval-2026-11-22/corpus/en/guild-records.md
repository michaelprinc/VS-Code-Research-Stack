# Fixture 07 — Guild records of the sandal makers (synthetic text)

The guild records in this fixture name twenty-two sandal makers working in one street. Apprentices served two
years, and a journeyman had to present one pair of sandals with a double stitch before being admitted.

The records distinguish two stitching patterns: the guild pattern, sewn from the inside of the sole, and the
household pattern, sewn from the outside. Admissions were judged on the guild pattern.

A later entry records that the guild kept a list of debtors and that three makers were removed from the roll
for missing two annual payments.
