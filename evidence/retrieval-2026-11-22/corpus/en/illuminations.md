# Fixture 06 — Illuminations of the invented Rajec codex (synthetic text)

The Rajec codex is an invented manuscript used only as fixture text for retrieval measurement. Its catalogue
entry records forty-two miniatures, sixteen of which show footwear: sandals whose straps cross over the instep
and whose heel is sewn from a single piece of leather.

The stitching technique is recorded as a double stitch with waxed thread. The catalogue notes that the artist
used a fine outline line only for footwear, which is how the footwear miniatures were identified.

The dating in this fixture is deliberately invented: the codex is listed as the fourth decade of the fourteenth
century, written in a Gothic minuscule with Czech interpolations.
