# Research workspace guidance

- Preserve original files under `papers/`; create derivative notes and reports under `notes/` or `outputs/`.
- When using an extracted document, cite its filename and page or paragraph locator. Distinguish source statements from interpretation.
- Report extraction limitations, including OCR needs, table/layout ambiguity, and missing text.
- Do not invent references or claim that NotebookLM, Copilot, GitHub, or another remote service was checked unless it was actually used.
- Keep credentials and machine-specific paths out of this workspace. Do not publish or push without the user's explicit request.
